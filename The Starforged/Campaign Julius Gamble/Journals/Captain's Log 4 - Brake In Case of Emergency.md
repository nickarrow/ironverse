*Aboard the wreck of the* Kilby, *a small holoprojector disk floated freely. Glinting in a ray of starlight that invaded the otherwise oppressive darkness of the room, the disc's slow rotation reveals a single word scratched into the metal of the disk's underside with a shaky hand:* MOIRAI. 

*Around it, only death and silence remained.*

---

The first warning buoy was a short ways behind the Silver Rose as its captain called his crew to the cockpit.

The little blue holo-sphere blinked to life, heralding Meg's presence. Bri was only a touch slower, walking in calmly with a large spanner over her shoulder and a oily cloth tucked into her belt.

"Are we there yet?" She quipped.

Ahead the massive spaceborne minefield loomed like a cloud of deadly dust and somewhere within it, their prize awaited. Gesturing at it, Gamble announced with a flourish "Feast your eyes, This is the exciting stuff right here!"

Bri was a little less enthusiastic about this progress, she offered a weak smile and turned her head to Meg
"Hey Meg, what can you tell us about this minefield?"

"Not much!"
She cheerfully replied. Just as Meg as about to let out an exasperated sigh, Meg continued.
"However, I do have some knowledge of spacefaring hazards. Generally speaking, most minefields are actually composed of multiple types of mines. The most commonly known mine associated with zero-G minefields is the classic contact mine."

Her holo blinked, now displaying a 3d rendering of a spheroid.

"The high explosive within is detonated whenever the mine successfully achieves a magnetic current with a passing ship. Shields are usually sufficient to maintain the distance required to keep the mine from going off."

With another blink of her holo, the spheroid now had small maneuvering thrusters.

"The next mine is sometimes called a seeker or chaser mine. These mines contain rudimentary heat seeking protocols that allow them to pursue anything larger than a snub fighter."

The holo blinked a third time, the spheroid now had a small cylinder inside that seemed to glow.

"And of course who could forget the pulse mine. These mines will interact with any shields in range, exploding in a EMP burst sufficient to knock out most shields for a short time. Of course when deployed alongside seeker and contact mines, even a few moments is more than sufficient to doom the vulnerable vessel."

The two humans in the cockpit slowly turned their gaze from the holo to the thousands of mines ahead of them. Meg took the opportunity finish her presentation on a far too cheery note
"The truly impressive thing is, from the exterior they're so indistinguishable that without the code used to deploy them a ship will never know if it's safe until the mines have atomized the intruder!"

The statement hung in the air as they processed the deluge of information. Gamble swallowed hard.

"Does this satisfy your request?"

Bri, stonefaced, uttered a deadpan "Yeah, that'll do."

The minefield remained suspended in space in front of them, passive and yet it began feeling more like a spider's web with each passing moment.

Bri turned to Gamble with a furrowed brow "Not gonna lie to you boss, I'm having second thoughts about this one."

Gamble met her gaze, a sympathetic expression on his face
"Look, I get that you're nervous. I am too, but I'm about to let our first adventure together be our last. Right now our first goal is just to find the *Kilby*. We can worry about getting to it after that."

Pulling up the data packet on his wrist computer, Gamble reviewed the data again.
"It says here that salvagers found a partially destroyed SOS buoy not too far from here. It stands to reason that the crew of the *Kilby* would have sent out more than one. If we can just find another one we may be able to extract a a point of origin from it and get our heading."
