Originally a luxury touring vessel used by an influential slum-lord on Taraxus, Julius came into possession of the ship after a large number of his disgruntled tenants stormed the slum-lord's secure compound while Julius was working there as security.

Speculation continues as to if the security system going down at that time was a coincidence or intentional, but it can safely be assumed that Julius won't be clarifying.

The ship already had a pretty healthy array of hidden compartments and amenities useful to someone operating less-than-legally, but it now boasts concealed external weaponry, an onboard armory, and several personalized accents.

The onboard AI, MEGARA, remains functional and largely untouched beyond a tweak to her vocal patterns to make her "More sultry."
