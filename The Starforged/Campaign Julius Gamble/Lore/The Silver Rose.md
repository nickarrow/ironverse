Originally named *The Yonder Star* and used as a luxury touring vessel by influential slum-lord [[The Starforged/NPCs/Julius Gamble's Minor Characters/Nix Cargor|Nix Cargor]] of Taraxus. Julius came into possession of the ship after a large number of Cargor's disgruntled tenants stormed the slum-lord's secure compound while Julius was working there as security. Speculation continues around if the security system going down at that time was a coincidence or intentional, but it can safely be assumed that Julius won't be clarifying.

Upon the ship's theft it already had a pretty healthy array of hidden compartments and amenities useful to someone operating less-than-legally, but Julius has made some tweaks to suit his purposes. It now boasts additional concealed external weaponry and a combat grade shield generator for when things get dicey out in the black. Despite this, it's still a ship built for touring the galaxy, consequently it still favors defense over offense and is only moderately armed for its size.

The onboard AI, MEGARA, remains functional and largely untouched beyond a tweak to her vocal patterns that according to Julius, make her sound "More sultry." She remains capable of performing the majority of ship support functions, allowing a single pilot to operate the ship with the help of her automation, albeit at a lower efficiency compared to a full crew compliment.
