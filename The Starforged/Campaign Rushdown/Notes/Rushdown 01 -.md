Rushdown enters [[Butcher]]'s office in [[Minami City]].  He's just arrived in the city today, after a long flight from [[Red Suns]] HQ. 

Butcher is standing at the window, facing away from Rushdown and staring towards the giant landed Ironhome that dominates the skyline.  He is wearing his full uniform despite the tropical heat.

"Captain Rushdown, reporting for duty, sir."

"Bizarre sight, isn't it?  Like a beached sea creature."  He waves theatrically with one arm and says "Take a seat.  Care for a drink?  Tea?  Something stronger?"

"No thank you, sir, my crew is eager to get to work so if we could just get our orders..."

"Orders?  Ha!"  Butcher finally turns around to face Rushdown, who is able to see that Butcher's helmet has been modified to include a grotesque ballistic mask in the shape of a skull.  "There are no orders.  We've been hired by [[Takamaki Heavy Industries|Takamaki]] to keep order in the sector and make sure that the [[Minami Clan]] keeps control.  But the Minamis don't know that they're being supported by Takamaki, so we're not receiving missions from them directly."

"No orders?  I don't understand."

"You'll just have to find your own missions.  I'll let you know if I get any contracts through the branch office here, but until then just fulfill the mission brief: Keep the peace and support the Minami Clan.  I'd suggest you get the lay of the land first, the key settlements are Minami City, Tranquilo, and Gallows.  Give each of them a visit and see if anything crops up."

"Understood, sir," says Rushdown.  He takes out his black iron blade.  "I vow to search the key settlements for any signs of trouble."

Rushdown was sure nobody wanted to miss out on a chance to sample the delights of the famed pleasure city for themselves.

They arrive safely at Tranquilo and dock, and the crew are in high spirits as they make their way ashore.  Rushdown stays aboard to look at the local 'net for any potential jobs.  There's an unexpected ping from his console - a frigate called the *Hidden Blade* is hailing the *Rude Awakening*.  Rushdown accepts the hail and a familiar face appears on the screen.

"It was only a matter of time before you showed up here," says [[Cassidy]], the Takamaki spy.

"Cassidy.  I should have killed you while I had the chance," replies Rushdown.

"Don't kid yourself; you never had the chance.  And besides, that's no way to speak to your new employer."

"Yeah, yeah.  What are you calling me for?"

"I was the one who recommended the Red Suns to Takamaki.  I'm the reason they requested you by name for this assignment.  Every victory for you makes me look good to my superiors.  You're putting your life on the line just to advance my career.  I just wanted you to know that."

"One day, I'll get the opportunity to settle our differences face to face, we'll see how smug you are then."

"Oh, I doubt that.  If this campaign is a success, Takamaki is going to acquire the Red Suns.  You'll be taking orders directly from me, in a Takamaki uniform."

"And if it's not a success?"

"Well, you'll be dead if that happens so it hardly matters one way or the other."

"Ok, you've had your gloat.  Any clues on where I should be looking?  My sector commander just told me to look busy, we don't have any work in the sector."
