*Rushdown has been tasked with getting rid of some [[Ironsail Raiders]] who are attacking ships near [[Tranquilo (City)]].*

`iv-oracle:Begin a Session|74|Unexpected return of an enemy or threat|move.oracle_rollable:starforged/session/begin_a_session.begin_a_session` 

Tranquilo City has two separate entertainment districts - one by the spaceport, known as Southside, and the main strip.  Southside is mainly for spacers and ship's crews, and is much rougher and seedier than the gleaming main strip where wealthy visitors enjoy the many delights of Tranquilo.

[[Skullcrusher]] is the leader of a mid-size gang in Southside.  His right hand man, [[Vagabond]], knocks on his office door and enters.

"Bad news, boss, one of our crews got hit."

Skullcrusher stands up and slams his metal claw into the desk.  "Who was it?  Another crew?"

"Some tourist.  Our guys robbed some guy in a uniform and then another guy in the same uniform shows up and kicks the shit out of them about an hour later."

"One guy?"

"One guy."

"Any bodies?"

"Nah, a couple of concussions and a fractured skull though."

"Get the word out to all our crews, anyone walking around our turf wearing that uniform gets a beatdown.  And try and find out who they were."

"You got it, boss," says Vagabond.

Rushdown heads to the bridge of the *Rude Awakening* and gives the order to move out toward the Ironsails' hunting grounds.  The crew are tense but professional and the ship glides out of the starport and breaks atmosphere, then the eidolon drive activates and they blink into FTL.

"We appear to be looking at two groups of small ships fighting, one side is outnumbered, but not outgunned" says Janus, the communications officer.  "Three attack ships taking on five civilian vessels."

"I'm guessing the attack ships are our Raiders but I want to be sure.  Fire a warning shot through the middle of the dogfighting then put me on broadcast on every frequency," says Rushdown.

The corvette's heavy cannon blasts through the throng of weaving craft, not hitting anything but surely making them take notice.

"This is Captain Rushdown of the Red Suns.  Everybody cease fire immediately and stand down or we will engage."

The three attack ships break off from the battle and start speeding towards the *Rude Awakening*.  The other five ships turn and take off in the opposite direction.  The first of the three ships is approaching firing range.

`iv-oracle:Combat Action|7|Change weapons or tactics|oracle_rollable:sundered_isles/misc/combat_action` 

The approaching ships start moving erratically, still moving toward the *Rude Awakening* but taking a wild and unpredictable path.

"I can't lock onto them; they're changing course too quickly," says Biggs, the gunnery officer.

The ships surround the *Rude Awakening*, maintaining their odd movement patterns.

"Can we hit them?" asks Rushdown.

"The turret guns can but we need more distance to use the heavy cannons," replies Biggs.

"We can't just sit here, there's no telling what they're planning," says Rushdown.  "Try and get us into a position where we can use our main guns on them."

The ship glides into motion and starts attempting to outmaneuver the three smaller ships.

`iv-move:Secure an Advantage|Edge|4|3|0|3|4|move:starforged/adventure/secure_an_advantage` 

The *Rude Awakening* flies forwards and performs a zero-gravity spin.  In an instant it is facing its previous position with all three smaller ships, presumably taken by surprise, in the firing arc of the main canons.  

The three ships each fire a barrage at the *Rude Awakening* and then slip into FTL before the corvette can reply.

"No hits," comes the reply from Rae, the chief engineer, in the engine room.  "But it looks like one of the power couplings has come misaligned from all these high-G maneuvers, we're going to have to fix it before we jump again."

"Understood, let me know when we're ready to go," replies Rushdown.  "They must have some kind of base nearby.  They've raided enough to fill those small ships several times over - they must have some kind of hauler hidden near here."

"I'll check my charts for likely hiding places nearby," says Scout, the navigator, already tapping instructions into his console.

The barman motions to a man drinking alone at a corner table at the back of the tavern.  Rushdown heads over and takes the seat opposite him.  Wulfric looks up in alarm at the heavily armored merc sitting across from him.

"You know who I am?" asks Rushdown.

"No idea," says Wulfric.

"You shot at my ship and then ran before I could shoot back."

"Oh, it's you.  What do you want?"

"I want you to stop raiding that spot."

"It's a good spot.  People head into Tranquilo loaded with goods and valuables to gamble away."

"And people who mess with the Angel Brothers' customers don't survive long.  There's other spots.  This one's not worth your life."
