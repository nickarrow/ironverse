Tranquilo City has two separate entertainment districts - one by the spaceport, known as Southside, and the main strip.  Southside is mainly for spacers and ship's crews, and is much rougher and seedier than the gleaming main strip where wealthy visitors enjoy the many delights of Tranquilo.

Skullcrusher is the leader of a mid-size gang in Southside.  His right hand man, Vagabond, knocks on his office door and enters.

"Bad news, boss, one of our crews got hit."

Skullcrusher stands up and slams his metal claw into the desk.  "Who was it?  Another crew?"

"Some tourist.  Our guys robbed some guy in a uniform and then another guy in the same uniform shows up and kicks the shit out of them about an hour later."

"One guy?"

"One guy."

"Any bodies?"

"Nah, a couple of concussions and a fractured skull though."

"Get the word out to all our crews, anyone walking around our turf wearing that uniform gets a beatdown.  And try and find out who they were."

"You got it, boss," says Vagabond.

Rushdown heads to the bridge of the *Rude Awakening* and gives the order to move out toward the Ironsails' hunting grounds.  The crew are tense but professional and the ship glides out of the starport and breaks atmosphere, then the eidolon drive activates and they blink into FTL.

"I'm guessing the attack ships are our Raiders but I want to be sure.  Fire a warning shot through the middle of the dogfighting then put me on broadcast on every frequency," says Rushdown.

The Corvette's heavy cannon blasts through the throng of weaving craft, not hitting anything but surely making them take notice.

"This is Captain Rushdown of the Red Suns.  Everybody cease fire immediately and stand down or we will engage."

The approaching ships start moving erratically, still moving toward the *Rude Awakening* but taking a wild and unpredictable path.

"I can't lock onto them; they're changing course too quickly," says Biggs, the gunnery officer.
