*Last time, Rushdown was tasked with tracking down the Miwako, the sister ship to the Minami.  Just as he set off to its last known location, a stowaway was found aboard the ship claiming to have a bomb.*

---

"You're the space scum working for the [[Minami Clan]].  Don't think you can scare me, I have a bomb.  If you try anything I'll blow a hole in the hull," comes a woman's voice from the other side of the doorway.

"Let's say I believe you," says Rushdown, "What are you waiting for?"

"Wiping you out is Plan B.  My mission is to hijack the ship and force you to fly to meet one of our people.  You rescued a Minami exec from Tranquilo and wiped out half a block doing it.  We want to know what was so important about him."

"I take it you're with the rebels?" asks Rushdown.

"The Resistance."

"Resistance.  Yeah that has a better ring to it.  Listen, I can't just take your word for it about the bomb, I'm going to have to scan you to make sure you're not bluffing."

"Try anything and I'll blow us all up."

"I'm going to have to risk it.  Janus," Rushdown says over comms, "Do a sweep for explosives aboard the ship."

"That wasn't part of our deal."

"What did you think I was going to do, let you stay on the bridge?"

They come out in deep space.  A multipurpose vehicle is sitting there waiting for them.

"Captain, they're hailing us," says Janus.

"On screen."

[[Captain Finn]]'s face fills the vidscreen, only...it's not quite Captain Finn.  His features are a little harder, and there's a glint of danger in his eyes.

"Where's Juliana?" he immediately asks.

"In a safe place."

"How did you get these coordinates?  Was she captured?  Tortured?"

"She gave herself up on the condition that you wouldn't be harmed or captured.  We haven't questioned her.  But I think introductions are in order, I'm Captain Rushdown."

"I'm Captain Finn."

"Cut the shit."

"It's true, all of the clones are called Captain Finn, after the late great last Minami captain.  But people nowadays call me The Spider."

"So what was it you wanted?"

"First I want proof that Juliana is alive and well."

"Janus, patch her through.  Voice only."

"Hmm?  What is it?" says Juliana over comms.  "Are we there yet?"

"Juliana, have they hurt you?" says The Spider

"No, they just locked me up."

"Did they interrogate you?"

"No, they promised not to harm me and they kept their word."

"Fine.  I'll talk to you after all this is over with."

"Janus, end it," says Rushdown and Juliana drops from the call.

"So, what did you want to talk to us about?" asks Rushdown.

"Who was the Minami man you killed a dozen men to save?  As far as we can find out he's a nobody."

"He is a nobody.  Minami didn't ask us to save him, they asked us to wipe out the gang who dared cross them.  I just saved him because the job would have felt half finished otherwise."

There's a pause.

"So I burnt my best undercover agent over a nobody?"

"It would appear so.  Look on the bright side, at least she didn't blow herself up over a nobody.  That's all you wanted to ask?"

"No, there was more but without the bomb threat I doubt you'll answer."

"Okay then."
