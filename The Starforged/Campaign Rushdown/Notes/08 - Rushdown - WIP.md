"They're Angels, at the end of the day," says Florian.

"We're the Angel Brothers.  It's our brand.  We can't just allow sisters to join after all this time."

"They're our blood, the DNA test confirms it."

"I'm not saying we don't look after them, I'm just saying they shouldn't be part of the business."

"Why not just give them something small to run and see how they handle it?  We don't need to give them a whole casino up front."

"Are you going to give up a piece of your business to gift them?"

"We can all chip in and start them off with a new venture."

"Well I think it's good for business.  It might open us up to entirely new clientele."

"Brothers, it's late," comes a voice from the end of the table.  The cacophony of voices stops.  "I don't think we're going to decide anything tonight.  Let's continue this discussion tomorrow."  The other Angels gradually file out of the room.  Florian waits behind to talk to the man at the head of the table.

"So what do you think?" he asks him.

"Like you said, they're Angels.  Don't worry, the others will come around.  Nobody wants to give up a piece of their cut, that's all.  It's always like this when a new sibling is found."  Florian nods.  "By the way," the man continues, "What went down in Southside?"

"A bunch of gangers decided to cross [[Minami Clan|Minami]]."

"And Minami went to these mercs instead of coming to us?"

"I think they wanted to give them a test, I don't think they meant any disrespect."

"Hmm.  What do you know about the mercs?"

"I used them to get rid of those [[Ironsail Raiders]] who were bothering our customers a while back.  They got the job done, quickly and quietly.  In their own way, mind you."

"So you'd vouch for them?"

"Too early to tell.  I sure as hell wouldn't count them out though."

There are multiple matches - various wrecks and derelicts have been found with these markings daubed on the walls and floors.  They are usually linked to massacres and ritualistic killings, and often drawn in blood.  It seems the Ascendancy, or another group like them, is still in operation to this day, although nothing is really known about them.  They show up and vanish seemingly at will.  There's no pattern to their attacks, they don't stay in once sector, and they don't take prisoners or loot.  They don't seem like a pirate band at all.

"I don't know who we're dealing with, but this doesn't feel like a cold case any more," says Rushdown.

"Just focus on the Miwako, we don't need to get involved with these guys unless they get in our way," says Gray.  "Oh, and I got a courier this morning; your new ship should arrive by the time you get back from Gallows."
