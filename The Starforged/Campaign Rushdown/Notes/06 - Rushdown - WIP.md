A few days later, Gray tells Rushdown to meet him at the Minami.  The arkship is the center of Minami City, having landed there over a century ago.  It towers over the other buildings in the city, and still provides most of the power to the settlement.  It is also the center of the Minami Clan's operations.

"We're going to meet [[Wynne Barrow]]," says Gray when they meet up.  "Apparently they have another job for us."

"Why does she want us to come to the Minami?  Usually it's Clan members only."

"She wouldn't say over comms.  I get the idea it's something big."

When they arrive they are given visitor's badges and leave their sidearms at the reception desk.  Security is tight, with armed guards on patrol throughout the interior of the ship.  Wynne Barrow comes down and meets them in the lobby.

"Sorry for the secrecy.  We're going to meet the Captain," she says.

Minami City is run by a clone of the original captain of the Minami.  The ship is in biometric lockdown, so only the original captain can operate the ship, but he's long dead.  So, the Minami Clan created clones who are able to get past the security systems.

Rushdown and Gray follow Wynne to the elevator and go up to the Captain's office.  The door is flanked by two guards.  Wynne flashes her ID and they stand aside to let the group pass.

The Captain is not what Rushdown expects.  He's not wearing his trademark Minami captain's uniform, instead he's wearing a baggy T-shirt, shorts, and slippers.  He's sitting on a sofa in front of a vidscreen playing some kind of game.  "Sorry, I'm online, can't pause," he yells.  After a few minutes, he finishes his game and comes over to the desk.  "Take a seat," he says to his guests.  "Do you want a drink or anything?"

"No, thank you, Captain," says Gray.  "Can I just say, it's an honor to meet you."

The Captain laughs.  "Everybody says that," he says.  "So, what do you know about the political situation in the sector?"

"Ashitaba belongs to the Minami Clan, run by yourself.  You have Tranquilo which the Angel brothers run and Gallows which is self governing, as well as a number of outlying settlements.  We heard something about rebels, but that's not common knowledge," replies Gray.

"Ha!  I don't run anything.  I'm very much a prisoner; I'm here because I'm the only one who can work the computer and the original captain went and died before he could tell anyone the password.  The Board make all the decisions.  Now, what I'm about to tell you doesn't leave this room.  The Minami didn't come here alone - it had a sister ship, the Miwako.  The plan was for the Minami to make planetfall and settle a city while the Miwako found a nearby asteroid belt to mine ores and minerals to be sent down to the main colony.  That is exactly what happened, for about 30 years.  Then, one day, the Miwako simply flew away.  Recently, someone was digging through old files and they found an old transmission from the day they left.  It turns out they reported receiving a distress call and went to investigate."

"And you want us to find the Miwako?" asks Rushdown.

"Well if it were that simple we'd send our own people.  Now, here's where the trouble begins.  The person who found that old file was...my brother.  The Clan creates batches of us clones, so they can still access the ship's computer if something happens to the acting Captain.  Anyway, he managed to escape the city somehow, possibly working with the rebels.  They want to find the Miwako hoping to dig up old dirt they can use to reveal the Clan's lies and undermine their authority enough to start a proper rebellion in Minami City.  Your job is to find the Miwako and secure any sensitive information it may hold."

Rushdown takes out his black iron blade.  Captain Finn's eyes widen.  "Knife!" he yells.  "Guards!"  Two guards burst through the doors, guns raised.  They point them at Rushdown.  He holds his hands up, still gripping the blade with his thumb.

"I was just going to swear an iron vow, I wasn't planning on using it," he says.

"Ah.  I see.  Of course," says Captain Finn, picking himself up from the floor behind the desk.  "False alarm, back to your stations," he says to the guards.

Everybody returns to their seats.

"I vow to discover the fate of the Miwako and recover any valuable information still available," says Rushdown.

The meeting over, Wynne Barrow escorts them back down to the lobby.

"He's quite different in person," says Gray.

"He's had extensive media training, he knows how to act in front of a camera," replies Wynne.  "Anyway, I expect you have a lot to do so I'll let you get on with it."

The two Red Suns exchange their visitor badges for their handguns and head back to Gray's office.  They scan the guns to see if Minami planted anything on the them but they're clean.

"This job may take you outside the sector," says Gray, "So it's time to let you in on a little secret.  HQ has a vested interest in this operation; there's a small navy on standby one sector over.  I can't have you taking part in inter-sector operations in a corvette, there's too much that can go wrong, so I'm going to call in a bigger ship for you.  The *Rude Awakening* will assume your patrol duties while you're on this mission, but it'll take a while for the new ship to get here.  Go and check out these coordinates, then report back."

"Why give me the new ship?  Why not just have its existing captain pursue the Miwako?"

"You were requested personally by Takamaki, we can hardly let you patrol Gallows and Tranquilo in a corvette while an operation of this scale is being carried out in the sector."

"Who will take over captaincy of the *Rude Awakening*?"

"Any preference?"

"I'd give it to Murphy."

"That can be arranged.  But don't say anything until you get back from this mission."
