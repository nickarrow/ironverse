*Rushdown has discovered that [[Butcher]], his sector commander, was an impostor.  While Butcher managed to get away, Rushdown has just gained access to his personal console and is going through the data.*

"Okay, the real Butcher is alive, it seems like they're keeping her in a safehouse here in the city," says Rushdown.  "I'm going there right away, you keep going through this data and find out who these guys are and where they're hiding."

"Don't you want me to come to the safehouse?  It'll be guarded," replies Murphy, the security officer.

"In the bedroom," says the man.  Rushdown kicks the door down and enters the room, gun raised.  He doesn't find any hostiles, but he does see Butcher chained to a radiator.

"Butcher?  Are you ok?" Rushdown asks Butcher.

"Just Gray is fine.  I'll be ok once I get out of here," Butcher replies.  Rushdown unchains her, and they make their way back to the office.  They gag the man and leave him chained to the radiator; they don't have a way to move him through the city without being noticed.  On the way, Rushdown gets Gray up to speed on what's been happening while she was being held hostage.

They arrive back at the office where Murphy is still going through the data on the terminal.
