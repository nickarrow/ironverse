*Rushdown has just fulfilled a mission for [[Florian Angel]] to stop a band of [[Ironsail Raiders]] from attacking civilians on their way to [[Tranquilo (City)|Tranquilo]].  Although the mission was successful, Florian was disappointed that Rushdown let the raiders live.*

`iv-oracle:Begin a Session|50|External factors create new danger, urgency, or importance for a quest|move.oracle_rollable:starforged/session/begin_a_session.begin_a_session` 

Wynne Barrow is standing in [[Butcher]]'s office in [[Minami City]].  She represents the Minami faction.

"So, Butcher, what brings you to Minami City?" she asks.

"The Red Suns are doing well for themselves, we're a growing organization.  We're looking to expand into this sector."

"Interesting.  Well, we may have some work for you.  A group of rebels oppose the powers that be in Minami.  Nothing major, isolated attacks on our ships mostly, a few raids on some of our other bases in the sector.  They have no hope of succeeding and there's an information blackout regarding them so most of our residents have never heard of them."

"That doesn't sound like anything major, surely your own forces should be able to take care of them?" says Butcher.

"Well yes, if we could find them.  They are rather elusive, you see.  There's an open bounty on information regarding them, and I thought the Suns might be interested in chasing it.  You wouldn't be the only people looking, of course."

"I'll have my people track them down.  I'll let you know as soon as we find something," says Butcher. 

"Do be careful, they're quite well informed and if they learn you're working with us you might end up as targets yourself."

"We can handle ourselves," says Butcher, his grin hidden from view by his facemask.

Rushdown is still on his mission to scope out the major settlements in the sector.  He decides to head into Tranquilo City to get a feel for the place.  He looks around Southside, the entertainment district by the spaceport, before heading to the main strip.

Southside is full of dive bars, with drunk spacers being loud and obnoxious wherever you look.  The strip is a stark contrast, resplendent casinos and entertainment halls vie for attention under the watchful eye of the Angel's enforcers.  Anyone making a scene is swiftly and discreetly dealt with.

When Rushdown gets back to the *Rude Awakening*, there's a [[Red Suns]] messenger waiting for him at the ship.  "Message from Butcher, sir.  He says you're to drop everything and head back to Minami City."

"Trouble?" asks Rushdown.

"I think it's a job."

Rushdown enters Butcher's office.

"Ah, Rushdown, just the man I wanted to see." says Butcher.

"The messenger said something about a job?"

"Yes, you can consider your previous mission completed, I want all your attention on this."

"Understood.  What's the job?" replies Rushdown.  Butcher gets him up to speed on the rebel attacks on Minami ships and bases.

"You're going undercover.  From sensor readings you'll look like a Minami transport, nobody will be able to see you're a warship until they're well within weapons range.  You're to fly between Minami outposts on the edge of the system until the rebels ambush you.  Try and take one alive for questioning, preferably an officer."

"It seems like a bit of a long shot.  What if they don't take the bait?" asks Rushdown.

"Our intelligence officers are hard at work, they should have a lead by the time you finish your patrol."

"I hope they do." Rushdown takes out his black iron blade once again.  "I vow to patrol the sector until we get ambushed or run out of places to look."
