Barely an hour later, Rushdown is walking towards the enemy base.  He has half a dozen Red Suns with him, as they are expecting heavy resistance.  Half a block further back are another half dozen gangers wearing sky blue colors.  The enemy base is a nightclub - the throbbing electro music leaks out from the deafening interior onto the street.

"The leader of the gang has an office past the VIP area.  We'll have to take him out to take out the gang.  We don't want to kill any civilians, so throw tear gas grenades as soon as we're through the door to clear the room." says Rushdown to his men.

The bouncer on the door, a large man with a purple tie, frantically starts speaking into his earpiece as the Suns approach.  As Rushdown gets closer he holds his hands up and says "Listen, you can't-"

He is cut off mid-sentence by Rushdown throwing a punch at his face.
