*9 years ago*
The icy wins of [[Rosston]] bit through the fur lined robes that [[Arana Haydar]] wore, as the storm raged across the frozen ocean called the Beria Wastes. 
The biting winds of Rosston tore across the Beria Wastes, the vast frozen ocean from which the Congregation's temple juts into the sky. Eight-year-old Arana trudged through the snow, wrapped tightly in the heavy, fur-lined robes typical of the Congregation members who live on the ice world. Even so, the thick layers were not enough to keep the planet's bitter cold at bay.  
Ahead of her, completely unaware he was being tailed, strode her father. Arana was pushing her nascent abilities, following his distinct signature in the Aether. But her little legs couldn't keep up with his long, purposeful strides. Slowly, his comforting presence in her mind's eye began to fade. Realizing she was falling too far behind, she finally turned around to head home—only to realize the blowing snow had swallowed her tracks. She had no way to find her way back.
Desperate, she turned back around and resumed following her father's fading path, hoping against hope she could catch up. But the distance grew too great, and his Aether signature blinked out entirely.
Just as true panic began to take root in her chest, a sound shattered the icy silence. A deep, bone-rattling howl echoed across the frozen plains, shaking the very world around her. It wasn't just an ice wolf—it was a direwolf. These massive beasts possessed a nearly human intelligence and their own dark connection to the Aether, viewing humans with disdain and frequently hunting them for the sheer challenge.  
Arana froze, her panic peaking. Then, reaching out into the Aether in absolute desperation, she felt it: a massive, thrumming signature radiating an overwhelming sense of warmth and protection. With the direwolves closing the distance behind her, she bolted to get away, heading blindly toward the comforting beacon.
She broke through the blinding snow and ran directly toward one of the Lode Mothers. These massive, ancient trees thrust their roots deep into the Aether ley lines running beneath the planet's skin, granting them a near-sentience and the ability to produce radiant heat. Exhausted and overwhelmed by fear, Arana collapsed at the base of the massive trunk and fainted.  
Sensing the child's distress, the Lode Mother took her in, hiding the unconscious girl safely within the shelter of its sprawling, warm branches. Moments later, the direwolves arrived. They paced at the edge of the Lode Mother's domain, clearly afraid of the immense, ancient presence of the tree. Growling in frustration, the intelligent predators reluctantly abandoned their hunt and slunk back into the blizzard.
Sometime later, Brodin was making his way back from a successful hunt, hauling the carcasses of three Crystal deer—crystalline-antlered herbivores that frequently travel between the Lode Mothers for food and warmth. As he walked by the massive tree, he stopped to look at it, only to spot a small, fur-clad bundle asleep against the trunk.  
Finding his daughter unharmed and breathing peacefully, he let out a profound sigh of relief. Glad she was alive and unhurt, he carefully scooped her up, securing her to his back to carry her home alongside his hunt.
As they journeyed back to the temple, the steady sway of his heavy steps roused Arana. She opened her eyes just enough to realize she was secure on her father's back. Knowing she was finally safe, she closed her eyes and went back to sleep.
