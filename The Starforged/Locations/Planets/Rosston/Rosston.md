Rosston is the icy home of [[The Congregation of Ethereal Paladins]].

The temple juts out of the frozen ocean known as the Beria Wastes

[[Arana Haydar]]'s homeworld.

This planet was once a paragon of life, but due to the malfunctioning precursor vault, it was turned into an icy Frozen ball. Much of the life went extinct, but some plants and animals have adapted to their much harsher environment.

There are massive trees scattered across the plains, called lode mothers. Their roots go deep into the ley lines of Aether running under the skin of the planet, and it gives them a connection to the Aether that gives them sentience. They are able to produce warmth by utilizing the power of the aether, and their branches produce a fruit in cycles that sustains the life of many herbivores that travel between the lode mothers for food and warmth.

One such species of these herbivores are similar to deer, they are called Crystal deer on account of their crystalline antlers and travel in herds.

One of the predator species is similar to a wolf, and are called ice wolves as a result, they have a connection to the Aether that aids them in their hunt for food. Members of the Congregation of Ethereal Paladins feel a kinship to them, and often take them as pets and companions on their travels.

While the ice wolves rarely preys on humans, their cousins the direwolves have no such conviction, and will frequently hunt any other living creature including ice wolves and humans. Direwolves are barely less intelligent than humans, and view humans with a disdain not easily shaken. It is generally frowned upon to kill a direwolf except in self-defense, as a result of their intelligence. The dire wolves also has a connection to the Aether, and are known to occasionally seek out ethereals to hunt them specifically for the challenge.
