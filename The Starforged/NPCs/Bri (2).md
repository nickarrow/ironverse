While outwardly friendly and good tempered with others, Bri's survival in the cut-throat business of salvaging indicates a much more calculating mind behind the friendly face. 

After being betrayed by her partners on a salvage job (who were never seen again) and left for dead, Bri was forced to stowaway on the *Valora's Comet*.  [[Jett Foley]] tracked her down as [[Jett 08 - Ghost Hunt|part of a job]] for the ship's captain, and rescued her from summary execution.  He dropped her off at [[Port Koshiba]] where she planned to join a ship's crew as an engineer.

Failing to find work as an engineer, Bri ended up returning to her former career of salvaging, until [[The Starforged/Campaign Julius Gamble/Characters/Julius Gamble|Julius Gamble]] was tipped off as to her skill and sought her out. She has agreed to travel with him for a time as his ship's engineer, but she continues to plan for the future.
