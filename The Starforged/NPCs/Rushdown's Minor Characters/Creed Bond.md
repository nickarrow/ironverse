Creed is a survivor of an [[Ascendancy of the Awakened Worlds]] attack decades ago.  He has become obsessed with them, and spends his life hoarding information about the group.  He is so dedicated that when he gets old, he clones himself and uploads his memories into a new body so he can continue his work after he's gone.  He currently has two bodies; one is 16 and one is 66.

He lives on [[Gallows]].
