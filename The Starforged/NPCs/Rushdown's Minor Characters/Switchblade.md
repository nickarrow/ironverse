
Callsign: Switchblade

Not much is known about Switchblade other than that he's a [[Satonaka Clan]] spy.  

He kidnapped [[Gray]] and impersonated her, and managed to get away before he got found out.  He may have been aboard the *Former Glory* when it was destroyed (see [[04 - Rushdown - Former Glory]]) but as nobody has seen his face it's impossible to tell if he was among the dead.
