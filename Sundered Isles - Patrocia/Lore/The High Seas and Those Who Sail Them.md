There are five commonly recognized seas in Patrocia today:

The Sea of Iron
The Frostborne Sea
The Seas of Zanthos
The Forsaken Sea
The Sea of Corsino

All are dangerous, none are empty.


# The Sea of Iron

A name used for the seas South of the Old World, the Sea of Iron used to be called the Silver Sea back in the day, but ever since the Rhodesian successor wars it has been a place of conflict and strife so great that its current name came into use. It is an excellent place to find salvage and riches if you have the skill and nerve to claim it.

If you choose to brave the Iron Sea, in addition to the regular beasts and hazards of the seas you should prepare to potentially encounter:

### The Undertow Corsairs
This band of ne'er do wells is a persistent threat in the Sea of Iron. Originally a loosely organized group looters and salvagers combing old battlefields, the eventually salvaged enough weaponry from sunken warships that they decide to stop waiting until ships sunk to take everything that wasn't nailed down. They're an unpredictable bunch of reprobates who will gladly rob you blind and leave you to row back to port in a life boat. They practically live aboard their ships, but rumors do persist that they must have a smugglers port somewhere.

### The Free Raiders
Silas Rooke was a captain in the Royal Hateshian Navy who served with distinction in over 15 years of war at sea. When his commanding officer finally retired, Rooke was passed over for promotion and an entirely unqualified noble's son was given the post instead. Rooke was aghast, but honored his duty. When that noble's son turned out to be wildly incompetent and corrupt, Rooke *still* honored his duty. But when this commander ordered him to keelhaul his first mate for speaking out of turn to object to the suicidal orders they were being given, something in Rooke snapped.
He keelhauled the commander instead, deserting with his men and beginning a campaign of vengeance against anyone and everyone who served any kind of nobility.

From his ship, *The Drowned Cur*, Captain Rooke has grown his cause and now commands a fleet of deserter ships that will ambush lone vessels, slay all aboard who don't desert to join them, and either commandeer the ship or send it to the bottom of the ocean.

### The 1st Hateshian Fleet
Led by Vice Admiral Skala Baro aboard the *IHN Imperious*, this fleet has seen many conflicts. 
Their standing duty is to hunt enemies of the emperor but they are free to interpret this creatively as the situation requires. For example, are you a merchant passing through their waters? This group might seize and search your vessel for "enemies of the emperor" without much fanfare. After some interrogation and maybe a small tax for wasting their time, you'll be allowed to continue your journey. The problem comes if you fly the flag of a nation not allied to Hatesh, or fail to fly a flag at all. At that point you're likely to become target practice for the gunners of these imposing warships.

### The 3rd Fleet of Michino
Michino's answer to the 1st Hateshan Fleet, the 3rd Fleet is led by Vice Admiral Sebastian Di Carrow from the *IFM Swift Accord*. Performing much the same functions as their rival, the 1st fleet, the 3rd is assigned to guard the southern coast of Michino from threats. While this ostensibly means pirates and deserters, the 3rd fleet skirmishes with the 1st fleet with some regularity. In fact, the two fleets have been at each other's throats for almost a full 70 years now, with varying levels of intensity over the years. The rivalry between the two most recent leaders is so well known at this point, they're sometimes referred to as The Dueling Vices.
