He walks into the tavern, and two men look up from the bar.  "Well, here he is, Erast's latest guildmaster," says one of the men.

"How's business?" says the other.  "You must be struggling if you can't even afford your own office."

"No, I'm sure there's plenty of work for a guild like his.  He'll put the ratcatcher out of work in no time."

"We're from the Blackfriar's Adventurer's Guild, here to check out the competition," says Billy Jones, "But there's nothing much to see."

"You don't seem that busy yourselves," says Johnny.

"Oh, don't worry about us, we have plenty of work.  We can still make time for social calls," says Billy Smith.

"And besides, we were just leaving anyway," says Billy Jones.  "You want my advice?  Pack your little guild in, you clearly don't have what it takes to make it work."

The two men swagger out of the tavern, heads held high.

"Don't mind them," says Sticky Joe.  "Nervous there might be some new competition, that's all."

"They're not wrong, though.  I've got no guild hall, no guild members, and no work.  Maybe this was a bad idea," says Johnny.

"It hasn't even been a week.  Give it time."

The next day, Johnny comes into the Theee Lillies at the normal time.  There's a man sitting in Johnny's usual seat at the bar.  He's thin and smartly dressed, and is casually eating an apple, carefully slicing pieces off with a switchblade.  The blade looks razor sharp.

Johnny walks in and takes the seat next to the man.  He orders a drink.  "You look like you're in here for a reason," he says.

"I'm here with a job for the guild.  If you're available."

"I'm Johnny Glass, I represent the guild."

"Ernesto Mason."

"So, what's the job?"

"There's a sea cave not far from here.  One particular passage is flooded.  I need a diver to explore the flooded passage and tell me what's on the other side."

"How do you know I'm a diver?"

"How did you fight off Sally Slaughter and her crew at the bottom of the ocean if you're not one?"

"Right.  What can I expect on the other side of this flooded passage?"

"Be ready for anything."

"How am I to see?  Wet torches don't light."

Ernesto's voice drops to a whisper.  "Take this ring.  When you can't see, put it on.  Don't let anyone know about it."

"Fine.  I'll check out your flooded passage.  By iron, I vow."
