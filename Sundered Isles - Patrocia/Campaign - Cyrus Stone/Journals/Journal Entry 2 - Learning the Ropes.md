It is the morning of my second day on this island where that which is lost at sea comes to rest. I took shelter in an outcropping of rocks for the night, covering myself with broad leaves from the nearby trees as a makeshift blanket. It was a restless sleep, the sounds of the jungle didn't didn't taper off with the daylight and a few times I thought I heard something rustling the bushes nearby. I am rising with the sun now, my luck held and nothing disturbed my ramshackle camp. I hope to find or create better shelter if I'm still out in the jungle come sundown tonight.

I have packed my meagre supplies up and take a swig of the rum I found yesterday. The fiery liquid burns on the way down, but it is a welcome sensation that helps bolster my alertness.
This journey could be long, and after yesterday's encounters I am certain I'll will need every advantage I can get against the things that lurk in the dark places of this jungle. 

Despite the rum, my still stomach rumbles. I need to hunt for food if I'm to sustain this pace. I resolve to keep an eye out for fruits, berries, nuts, or prey that might sustain me as I explore today.
With that plan in mind, I enter the brush.
