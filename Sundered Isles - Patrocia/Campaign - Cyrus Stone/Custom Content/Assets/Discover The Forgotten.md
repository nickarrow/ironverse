# Discover The Forgotten (Motivation)

You live to discover forgotten lore and uncover secrets.
Buyoff: When you willingly pass up an opportunity to learn something important or get a forgotten item.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you learn what needs to be done to discover an important new fact, a lost piece of information, or lost item.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you discover an important new fact, a lost piece of information, or lost item.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important to discover an important new fact, a lost piece of information, or lost item.
