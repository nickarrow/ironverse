# Champion (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
You are proud of your personal capability to achieve victory.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you perform a considerably impressive deed worthy of being retold.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble for not acknowledging someone else’s deeds, or as being your superior.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you abandon a simple task beneath your capabilities.
