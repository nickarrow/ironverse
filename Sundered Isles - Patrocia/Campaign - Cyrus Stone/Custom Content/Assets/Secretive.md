# Secretive (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Shadow 2+
You conceal information from others.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you act to hide information that would benefit others.
* [ ] **Vice:** When complications result from your unwillingness to share information you are known to possess.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you decide to share information you had previously concealed.
