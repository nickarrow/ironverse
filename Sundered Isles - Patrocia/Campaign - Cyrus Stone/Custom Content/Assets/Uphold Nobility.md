# Uphold Nobility (Motivation)

You believe yourself to be a cut above the common people.
Buyoff: When you show that you have gotten over yourself by denying any noble trait by act or words.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you demonstrate superiority by refusing to stoop to the level of the ones you think beneath you.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you, and the ones with you, benefit from your showcase of nobility.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When your noble traits overcome a problem.
