# Prove Myself (Motivation)

You are looking to prove yourself worthy.
Buyoff: When you willingly pass up an opportunity to test yourself, decline a challenge, or give up on a challenge.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you take on a difficult challenge.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you succeed at overcoming a challenging obstacle in order to accomplish a goal.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you fail at a significantly risky test, but persevere and try again.
