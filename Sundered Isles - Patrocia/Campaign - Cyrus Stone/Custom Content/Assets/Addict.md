# Addict (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
You have a fix, and whatever it is, you feel the urge to have it.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you perform an action that would directly lead you to consume your fix, or when you act under its influence in a charged situation.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble for making an unwise decision influenced by your eagerness to consume your fix.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you willingly pass up the opportunity to consume your fix, getting over your addiction.
