# Protective (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Iron 2+
You have an ingrained desire to protect your friends from harm.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you act to provide safety or security for someone else.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in a very delicate situation for acting aggressively overprotective for the actual situation.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you willingly put your own safety over the safety and security of those that need your protection.
