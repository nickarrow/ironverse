# Callous (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
You can separate yourself from your emotions towards anything or anyone.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you act cold-heartedly in a situation in which efficiency depends on it.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble for acting ruthlessly when a more compassionate manner is either expected or required.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When your feelings are moved by something or someone, and you act accordingly.
