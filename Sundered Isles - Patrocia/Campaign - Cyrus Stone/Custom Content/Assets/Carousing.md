# Carousing (Motivation)

You live for making use of you wealth and time to yourself, or others, feel good.
Buyoff: When you refuse, or argue against, using your valuable time to raise spirits, yours or someone else's.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you spend your wealth or valuable time to raise your spirits.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you spend your wealth or valuable time to raise someone else's spirits.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you postpone or decline a duty in order to spend wealth or valuable time raising spirits, yours or someone else's.
