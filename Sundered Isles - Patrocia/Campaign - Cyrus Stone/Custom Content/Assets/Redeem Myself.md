# Rebel (Motivation)

You are workings towards rebuilding trust and righting past wrongs.
Buyoff: When you have rebuilt your reputation or give up on the path to redemption.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you act to rehabilitate your reputation.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you prove yourself to someone who doubts or thinks poorly of you.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important, by acting to prove yourself to someone who doubts or thinks poorly of you.
