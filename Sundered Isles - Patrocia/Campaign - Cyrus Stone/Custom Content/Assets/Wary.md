# Wary (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
You always take the safest path or choose the safest course of action.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you act in order to take precautions.
* [ ] **Vice:**  When you get into trouble or a risky situation for trying to avoid danger.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you face a threat directly without considering the consequences.
