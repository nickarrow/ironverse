# Sensual (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
You take particular delight in the gratification of your senses.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you consume something or participate in an activity that causes you pleasure.
* [ ] **Vice:** When your insatiable appetite or lack of moderation in your pursuit of self-indulgence brings complications.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you willingly abstain from a pleasurable activity when your spirit needs it.
