# Pursue Peace (Motivation)

You abhor violence.
Buyoff: When you willingly resort to violence or allow someone else to do so.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you act to show the importance of pacifism.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you diffuse a violent situation or prevent someone from resorting to violence.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you refuse to, or avoid, hurting someone else in a situation that demands it because you or someone else was at risk.
