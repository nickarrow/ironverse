# Classy (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Heart 2+
You always look to dignify your persona, measuring yourself and others by tastes and decency, both in looks and manners.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you leverage your mannerism or good-looking to take advantage in a situation that requires it or in which is influential.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble for acting arrogantly as per your high-class mindset.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you disregard by act the idiosyncrasy of refined mannerisms.
