# Rebel (Motivation)

You despise structures that support formal authority among individuals.
Buyoff: When you acknowledge, support, or become an authority, or accept a favor from one.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you disobey an authority.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you act against an authority.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important, by acting against an authority.
