# Humorous (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Heart 2+
You tend to find the humor in every situation.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you come up with a prank, joke, pun, or trick in an attempt to garner goodwill, make a friend, or lift someone’s spirit.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble for your lack of seriousness in a charged situation.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you don’t attempt to bring joy in a time of sorrow.
