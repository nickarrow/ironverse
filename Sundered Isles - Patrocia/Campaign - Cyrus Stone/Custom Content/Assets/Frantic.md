# Frantic (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Edge 2+
You are always on the go, acting on instinct, without much thought or
consideration.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you act quickly to get things done in a situation in which time is crucial.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you face the consequences of not being patient enough or promoting recklessness.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you patiently consider your steps in a situation in which time is crucial.
