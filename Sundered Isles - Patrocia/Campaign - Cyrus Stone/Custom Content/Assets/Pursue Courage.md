# Pursue Courage (Motivation)

You encourage bravery and ensure it is present whenever it is up to you.
Buyoff: When you allow fear to overcome you, or give up on and important task because it would be too dangerous to proceed.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you act despite a terrifying obstacle.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you instill confidence in someone else and it pays off favorably.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When your words or deeds fill others with courage and it pays off favorably.
