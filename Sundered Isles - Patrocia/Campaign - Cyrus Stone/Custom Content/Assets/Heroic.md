# Heroic (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Heart 2+
You try to save others by living up to the glorious ideal of confronting evildoers.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you directly aid someone who is clearly a victim of evil.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get into a delicate situation due to heroic impulses or jumping to a false conclusion about a situation.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you commit an evil act against someone or willingly refuse to aid an obvious victim of evil.
