# Curious (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Wits 1+
You are always eager to learn and on the lookout for the new or mysterious.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you indulge your urge to learn something when it is uncertain it will help your quest.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble for following your impulses to learn and experience the new or mysterious.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you willingly restrain your impulses to learn and experience the new or mysterious in a situation when it is not risky to do so.
