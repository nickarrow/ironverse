# Opportunistic (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Shadow 2+
You are quick to adapt your actions based on what is expedient or effective, regardless of ethical principles.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you utilize an ethically questionable approach to obtain a benefit or resolve a situation.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get into trouble for your unethical approach to a situation.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you willingly refrain from taking the most expedient or effective course of action because it is unethical.
