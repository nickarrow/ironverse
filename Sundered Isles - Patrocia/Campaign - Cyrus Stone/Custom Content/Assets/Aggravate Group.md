# Aggravate Group (Motivation)

You spurn an organization or community for ideological reasons. 
Buyoff: When the group you condemn either cease to exist, or you support it.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you speak in public or show how to act against the group you condemn
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you act against the group you condemn
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important, for acting against the group you condemn.
