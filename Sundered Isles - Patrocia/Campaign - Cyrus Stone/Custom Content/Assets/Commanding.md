# Commanding (Motivation)

You are looking to stand out as an exemplary leader.
Buyoff: When you willingly abdicate your leading position.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you act according to the fact that you are in command
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you issue and order to someone, get them to follow it, and it pay off favorably.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you issue and order to a group, get them to follow it, and it pays off favorably.
