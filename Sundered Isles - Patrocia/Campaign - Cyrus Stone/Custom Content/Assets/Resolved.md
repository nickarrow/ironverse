# Resolved (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
You stay true to your decisions no matter what.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you swear a vow, and when you take a decisive action to reach a milestone on a formidable, extreme, or epic one.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you stubbornly decide to follow a path proven to be wrong or suboptimal for the desired goal.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you forsake a vow or abandon a declared course of action.
