# Cynic (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
Your low expectations for life give you a bitter attitude toward people and your surroundings.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you act to protect yourself from being disappointed, or under the assumption that you will be.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble because of your refusal to make an optimistic decision or assumption.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you express sincerely optimistic behavior while engaged in an endeavor.
