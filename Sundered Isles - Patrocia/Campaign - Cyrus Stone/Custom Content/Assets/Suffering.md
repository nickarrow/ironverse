# Suffering (Motivation)

You thrive on personal pain and martyrdom.
Buyoff: When you willingly flee from a source of physical or mental damage.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you willingly suffer physical or mental damage.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you willingly suffer physical or mental damage and your next move shows you are not fleeing from the source of that damage.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you get wounded or shaken.
