# Gambler (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
Your randomizer is your most prominent sidekick, be it a coin, a dice, or something else.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you decide by chance a course of action to follow, and take the first steps to run with it.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you face the consequence of previously deciding a course of action by chance.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you take time to seriously consider all your options before making a frivolous decision.
