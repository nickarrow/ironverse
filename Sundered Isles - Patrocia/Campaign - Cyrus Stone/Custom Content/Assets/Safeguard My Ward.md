# Safeguard My Ward (Motivation)

You are looking to provide security and protection to an individual at all costs.
Buyoff: When your ward dies or you act as though protecting this person is not your priority, or is not your duty.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you prevent your ward from doing something that would put them at risk.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important, by protecting your ward.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you rescue your ward from potential harm.
