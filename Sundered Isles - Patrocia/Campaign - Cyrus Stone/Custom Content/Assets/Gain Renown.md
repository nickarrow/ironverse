# Gain Renown (Motivation)

You are looking to make a name for yourself.
Buyoff: When refuse to take credit for an accomplishment, or allow someone else to claim credit for something you accomplished.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you do something to impress someone else.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you get prestige or take credit for an action, regardless of if you did it or not.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important to impress someone, or as a consequence for taking credit for an action, regardless of if you have did it or not.
