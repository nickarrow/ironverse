# Irascible (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Iron 1+
You have a menacing and savage mien, and could explode at the slightest provocation.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you apply your savagery as the most efficient way to accomplish your action.
* [ ] **Vice:**  When you get into trouble for allowing your gruffness or anger to ruin an opportunity or situation.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you show self-control and act in a delicate manner in a situation that would normally trigger your aggressiveness.
