# Subversive (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Shadow 1+
You are an independent thinker who tends to oppose the establishment.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you act to abolish an individual, group, or idea.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble for disrespecting an authority or not sticking to a previous plan but following your own course of action.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you pledge to an individual, group, or idea and fulfill your commitment to the end of an assigned task.
