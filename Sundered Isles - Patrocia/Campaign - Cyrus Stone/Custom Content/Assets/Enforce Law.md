# Enforce Law (Motivation)

You champion lawfulness and make sure the law is fulfilled whenever it is up to you.
Buyoff: When you break the law or allow someone else to break it.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you act to uphold the law.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you act to prevent a crime committed by someone else.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important by acting to uphold the law or prevent a crime committed by someone else.
