# Uncover My Past (Motivation)

You are looking to retrieve information from your past.
Buyoff: You have uncovered all you needed to know from your past, or let it go.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you get to a moment with the potential to give you something from your past.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you learn or discover something from your past.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important, for the sake of learning or discovering something important from your past.
