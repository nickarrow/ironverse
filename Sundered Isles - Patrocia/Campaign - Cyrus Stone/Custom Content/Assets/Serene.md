# Serene (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Heart 1+
You are difficult to anger and easily shrug off any negative emotion that might burden your heart.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When your composure protects your emotional well-being or that of another.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you end up in a risky situation due to not being forceful enough.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When a situation triggers in you strong feelings of impatience or anger.
