# Cover My Past (Motivation)

Your past is something you are looking to hide from certain individuals.
Buyoff: When you willingly reveal your past to whom you were not interested in revealing it, or they discover it by other means.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you make preparations to cover your past.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you cover your past in a situation that demands it be revealed.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you cover your past by convincing others despite suspicions.
