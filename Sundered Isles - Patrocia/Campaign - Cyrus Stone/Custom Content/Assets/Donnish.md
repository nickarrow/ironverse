# Donnish (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Wits 2+
You tend to spread your knowledge to those around you.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you share your knowledge on a matter so it can be used in a practical way.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble for your pretentiousness in your knowledge or revealed lack of.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you intentionally let others know your lack of knowledge in an area you are supposed to know.
