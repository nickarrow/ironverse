# Schemer (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Shadow 3+
You are adept at deceit and getting things done furtively, mostly using other people.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you incite someone else to do something by appealing to their emotions.
* [ ] **Vice:**  When you get into trouble because your deception is revealed, get caught lying, or are forced to pay for your previous deceit.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you choose to act honestly and reveal your previous deceit.
