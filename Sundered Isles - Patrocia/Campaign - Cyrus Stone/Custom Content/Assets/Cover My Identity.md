# Cover My Identity (Motivation)

Your real identity is something you are looking to hide from certain individuals.
Buyoff: When you willingly reveal your identity to whom you were not interested in revealing it, or they discover it by other means.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you make preparations to cover your identity.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you cover your identity in a situation that demands it be revealed.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you cover your identity by convincing others despite suspicions.
