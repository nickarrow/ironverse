# Empathetic (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Heart 2+
You have the capability to easily put yourself in others’ shoes.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you use your capabilities to understand and share others’ feelings in order to connect with them.
* [ ] **Vice:** When your empathy causes issues that could have been avoided with a harder stance.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you willingly disregard the feelings of another, despite it being expected of you.
