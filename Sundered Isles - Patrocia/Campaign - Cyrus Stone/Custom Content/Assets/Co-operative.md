# Co-operative (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Heart 1+
You are quick to see the importance of working with others.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you make an action in direct support of someone.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you put yourself at risk by making a decision or acting in a way that shows your dependence on others.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you willingly decide to act alone in circumstances in which company is beneficial.
