# Intrepid (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Heart 3+
You always advance, even in the face of unimaginable danger, unafraid to fight at a moment’s notice.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you rush to action or step into the unknown without much consideration.
* [ ] **Vice:**  When you get into trouble as a result of your unhesitating leap into a noticeably risky situation.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you decide to not leap headlong into a perilous situation.
