# Acquisitive (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
You don’t resist any chance of getting hold of any material goods.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you act to directly obtain some wealth or material things.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble for getting wealth or material things from someone else or for acting thrifty.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you give a considerable portion of your wealth to someone else.
