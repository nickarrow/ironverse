# Preserve Nature (Motivation)

You desire to preserve the natural world.
Buyoff: When you cause damage to a natural element (accidentally or not) or allow someone else to.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you act to preserve nature.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you prevent someone else from causing harm to a natural element.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important to prevent someone else from damaging nature, or by offering aid to an element of nature.
