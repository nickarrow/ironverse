# Critic (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Wits 3+
You believe things should not be accepted without examination, which often makes you find imperfections in everything.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you act to expose or detect a flaw and make use of it.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get into trouble for acting under the assumption that there’s a flaw.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you accept something without examining or questioning its quality or efficiency when a critical eye is required or necessary.
