# Zealot (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
You believe with absolute faith in the precepts of your ideology, god, or leader with deep convictions.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you draw strength from your absolute faith or loyalty while in a charged situation that you can relate to your ideology, god or leader.
* [ ] **Vice:**  When complications result from you dogmatically following your loyalty or faith.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you disregard your convictions by acting contrary to the teaching of your ideology, god or leader.
