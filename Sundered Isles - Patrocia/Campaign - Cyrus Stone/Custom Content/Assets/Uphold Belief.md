# Uphold Belief (Motivation)

You have a strong conviction that guides you and you want to give testimony of it to others.
Buyoff: When you renounce your belief, speak against it, or show that sharing it is no longer important to you.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you explain or defend your belief to someone else.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you convince someone else of your belief.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk or sacrifice something important, by upholding your belief.
