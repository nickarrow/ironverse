# Analytic (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Wits 3+
You tend to let your mind do the work of understanding intricate issues whenever there is the chance.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you use a logical or systematic approach to understand or solve a problem, or when you look to get information to be used in the future.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you get in trouble for acting according to a wrongly over-analyzed situation.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you willingly let a puzzle go unsolved despite having the certainty it will negatively pay off.
