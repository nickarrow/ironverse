# Pursue The Truth (Motivation)

You ensure the truth will come out and champion it whenever it is up to you.
Buyoff: When you lie or suppress the truth from coming out.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you ensure the truth comes out in a charged situation.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you uphold the truth in spite of opposition.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important, by upholding the truth.
