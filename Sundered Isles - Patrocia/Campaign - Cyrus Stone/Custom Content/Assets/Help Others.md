# Help Others (Motivation)

You feel inclined to help those in need.
Buyoff: When you willingly ignore a request for help or the fact that someone evidently needs it.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you help those who cannot help themselves.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you put yourself in danger, or sacrifice something important, in order to help someone else.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you take someone in unfavorable circumstances and change their life to the point they can help themselves.
