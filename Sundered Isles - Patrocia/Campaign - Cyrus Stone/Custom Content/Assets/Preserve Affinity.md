# Preserve Affinity (Motivation)

You have someone you are friend or kindred to, and whom you are strongly interested in keeping close. 
Buyoff: When your relationship with your friend or kindred is severed, or you do something to show that the relationship is no longer a priority to you.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you act to leverage your affinity with your friend or kindred.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you must make an important decision that is influenced by your friend or kindred.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you put yourself at risk, or sacrifice something important, by being loyal to your friend or kindred.
