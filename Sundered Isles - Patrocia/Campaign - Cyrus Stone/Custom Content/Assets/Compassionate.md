# Compassionate (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Heart 2+
You show selfless concern for others, actively caring for the welfare of those in need and aiding them.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you nurture someone else, or act with mercy.
* [ ] **Vice:** When you face the consequences of giving others the chance to take advantage of your compassion.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you don’t show mercy in a situation as is expected or required of you.
