# Imposing (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Iron 3+
You make sure your stately and proud presence doesn’t go unnoticed.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you act with a regal mien over others to get things done or intimidate.
* [ ] **Vice:**  When you get in trouble for acting in an imperious manner.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you willingly submit to someone else’s command when you are absolutely certain others would benefit from your leadership.
