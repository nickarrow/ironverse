# Pursue Justice (Motivation)

You ensure that your sense of fairness comes to fruition whenever it is up to you.
Buyoff: When you commit to a morally questionable act, allows someone who deserves judgement to avoid it, or stand mute before injustice.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you successfully settle a conflict in a way that results in a just outcome.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you successfully settle a conflict in the presence of someone who differs from your sense of fairness.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When you bring someone to justice who deserves it.
