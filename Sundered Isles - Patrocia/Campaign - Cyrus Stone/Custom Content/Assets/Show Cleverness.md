# Show Cleverness (Motivation)

You like using your intelligence to enrich your interactions and solve problems.
Buyoff: When your refuse to share knowledge (practical or otherwise) or refuse to provide your insight when others demand it.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Layer 1:** When you come up with a clever plan or point out something no one else knew.
* [ ] **Layer 2:** When you point out something no one else knew and it pays off favorably.
* [ ] **Layer 3:** When a plan previously elaborated by you pays off.
