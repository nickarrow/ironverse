# Stoic (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: Heart 1+
You believe all suffering must be endured calmly and without grumbling.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you suffer a considerable physical, mental or spiritual hardship without complaining.
* [ ] **Vice:** When your willingness to keep enduring hardship gets you into a very delicate situation.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you act in a way that shows that you are not willing to endure hardship when you could, or when you show signs of suffering.
