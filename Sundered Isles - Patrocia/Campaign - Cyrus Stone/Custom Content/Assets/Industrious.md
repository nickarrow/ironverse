# Industrious (Demeanor)

Stat Requirement: None
You are diligent, hardworking, and don’t hesitate to assume important challenging tasks.
## Abilities

* [ ] **Virtue:** When you proceed to diligently take over a noticeably difficult endeavor.
* [ ] **Vice:**  When you decide to assume a demanding endeavor despite you not being in the best position to do so, or when your restless efforts and hard work puts you in a delicate situation.
* [ ] **Buyoff:** When you willingly take time to relax and remain uninvolved in charged circumstances.
