Second among the Great Houses was House Vincelli. They were to advance the culture and science of the empire, and ensure the prosperity of Rhodesia for eons to come. The advances and wonders they created and spread are among the most sought after relics of the old empire.

In the Modern Age House Vincelli are led by Lord Antonio Vincelli, and they produce some of the greatest artists and engineers of the modern age, though the lost mysteries of discoveries past still haunt them.

Lord Antonio is reputed to be an eccentric and refined man, if scatterbrained at the best of times. He's considered a valuable ally, but he's hard to convince due to his aloof nature.
